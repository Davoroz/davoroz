Biography

I studied economics at the National University of Colombia in Bogotá. Placing first in the XX National Economics Olympiad, organised by the National Federation of Economics Students (FENADECO), earned me a full scholarship for graduate study, and I went on to complete an MSc in Economics at ICESI University in Cali.

Along the way I worked as a research intern at Finagro, forecasting agricultural commodity prices, and at the Central Bank of Colombia, where I helped build a new indicator of inflation perceptions from more than 80 million tweets. At ICESI I spent two years as a research assistant in health economics, building data pipelines and using machine learning to detect anomalies in healthcare billing.

A Global Affairs Canada research scholarship brought me to Dalhousie University as a research visitor through the Emerging Leaders in the Americas Program, where I studied price rigidities with high-frequency data. I have since returned to Dalhousie for the Master in Development Economics, supported by the Professor George A.B. Kartsaklis Memorial Scholarship.
